Features

Why BoneFish?

Everything you need to enhance, customize, and optimize your Roblox experience in one powerful app.

🎮

Multi-Instance Launcher

Run multiple Roblox accounts simultaneously with smart window management and per-instance settings.

⚡

FPS Unlocker

Break past Roblox's 60 FPS cap for buttery-smooth gameplay. Real-time FPS counter included.

🎨

Theme Engine

Customize every pixel with built-in dark/light themes, custom bootstrapper styles, and community themes.

🏴‍☠️

FastFlags Manager

Full graphical editor for Roblox FastFlags. Enable FPS unlock, performance tweaks, and hidden features.

📦

Mod Support

Import community mods with one click. Custom cursors, fonts, sounds, UI themes, and more.

🔄

Auto-Reconnect

Never lose progress with smart reconnection. Auto-join back to servers when disconnected.

🔒

Account Switcher

Quickly switch between Roblox accounts with cookie-based management. No more logging in repeatedly.

🎯

Crosshair Overlay

Customizable crosshair overlay for any game. Multiple styles, colors, and opacity settings.

🧹

Cache Cleaner

One-click Roblox cache cleaning. Free up gigabytes of disk space and fix common issues.
